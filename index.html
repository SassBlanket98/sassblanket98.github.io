Multi-Tenant AI Gateway

Client deployment · one gateway, two companies, zero shared context

A multi-agent deployment standing up department-facing AI agents for two independent companies that share an office and a Slack workspace, but must never share data. The hard part is holding two companies' worth of client and operational data on one gateway and proving, not just documenting, that one company's business can never leak into the other's context. Giving an agent memory is easy. Proving isolation is structural is the work.

OpenClaw Multi-Agent Tenant Isolation MemPalace / ChromaDB Slack + ClickUp
4 agents (2 live, 2 designed) 2 tenant companies Schema-level isolation
//Isolation is structural: forbidden tools are absent from an agent's schema, not blocked by a policy it could reason around.
