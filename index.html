Multi-Tenant AI Gateway

Client deployment · one gateway, two companies, separately scoped agents

This gateway runs separate department-facing AI agents for two independent companies that share an office and a Slack workspace. Each agent's tools and data access are scoped to its own role and tenant, not shared by default. That scoping isn't a solved, closed problem: it's something I keep testing and tightening as gaps turn up, not a claim that crossover is impossible.

//Each agent's tool schema only includes what its role needs, so a tool it shouldn't use isn't offered as an option, rather than relying on a policy telling it not to.
//Department agents report to the teams that own the work, not back through the operator.
//Scope changes go through review before they ship, since this is the boundary that matters most if something goes wrong.
